[GOALS]
GOAL-001:
  Description: Achieve glycemic control meeting ACOG/ADA targets
  Target Value: Fasting <95 mg/dL, 1-hr PP <140 mg/dL, 2-hr PP <120 mg/dL, >=80% values at target
  Target Days: 14
  Priority: HIGH
  Guideline: ACOG Practice Bulletin 190, ADA Standards of Care 2026

GOAL-002:
  Description: Classify GDM as GDMA1 or GDMA2 based on response to lifestyle modification
  Target Value: Determine if >20% glucose values out of range after 1-2 weeks MNT
  Target Days: 14
  Priority: HIGH
  Guideline: ACOG/SMFM Classification Criteria

GOAL-003:
  Description: Prevent fetal macrosomia through glycemic control
  Target Value: Estimated fetal weight <4000 grams at delivery
  Target Days: 280
  Priority: HIGH
  Guideline: ACOG Fetal Growth Monitoring Guidelines

GOAL-004:
  Description: Implement appropriate fetal surveillance based on GDM class
  Target Value: GDMA1 no routine testing; GDMA2 testing at 32 weeks
  Target Days: 224
  Priority: HIGH
  Guideline: SMFM Choosing Wisely, ACOG Practice Bulletin 190

GOAL-005:
  Description: Achieve appropriate delivery timing by GDM class and control status
  Target Value: GDMA1 39-40+6wk; GDMA2 good 39wk; GDMA2 poor 37-38+6wk
  Target Days: 280
  Priority: HIGH
  Guideline: ACOG Delivery Timing Recommendations

GOAL-006:
  Description: Complete postpartum glucose tolerance testing
  Target Value: 75-gram OGTT completed between 4-12 weeks postpartum
  Target Days: 84
  Priority: HIGH
  Guideline: ACOG/ADA Postpartum Diabetes Screening

GOAL-007:
  Description: Establish long-term diabetes prevention plan
  Target Value: Lifelong screening every 1-3 years, lifestyle intervention if prediabetes
  Target Days: 365
  Priority: MEDIUM
  Guideline: ADA Diabetes Prevention Program Standards

[INTERVENTIONS]
INT-001:
  Type: PROCEDURE
  Description: GDM screening with 50-gram glucose challenge test at 24-28 weeks
  Procedure Code: 82950
  Schedule Days: 0
  Instructions: Non-fasting; draw blood 1 hour post; threshold 130-140 mg/dL per institution
  Guideline: ACOG Universal Two-Step Screening

INT-002:
  Type: PROCEDURE
  Description: Diagnostic 100-gram 3-hour OGTT if screening positive
  Procedure Code: 82951
  Schedule Days: 7
  Instructions: Fasting 8+ hours; Carpenter-Coustan criteria; diagnose if >=2 abnormal values (FPG>=95, 1hr>=180, 2hr>=155, 3hr>=140)
  Guideline: ACOG Carpenter-Coustan Diagnostic Criteria

INT-003:
  Type: EDUCATION
  Description: Medical nutrition therapy initiation within 1 week of diagnosis
  Procedure Code: 97802
  Schedule Days: 0
  Instructions: RDN consultation; carb counting 175-200g/day; 3 meals + 2-3 snacks; individualized plan
  Guideline: ADA Medical Nutrition Therapy Guidelines

INT-004:
  Type: LIFESTYLE
  Description: Physical activity prescription for 150 minutes per week
  Schedule Days: 0
  Instructions: 30 min moderate activity 5 days/wk; walking after meals; avoid supine exercise after 1st trimester
  Guideline: ADA Physical Activity Guidelines

INT-005:
  Type: MONITORING
  Description: Self-monitoring blood glucose 4 times daily for classification
  Schedule Days: 0
  Instructions: Fasting + 1-2 hr post each meal; record in log; assess after 1-2 weeks for A1 vs A2 classification
  Guideline: ACOG/ADA Glucose Monitoring for Classification

INT-006:
  Type: MONITORING
  Description: Control assessment at 1-2 weeks to classify GDMA1 vs GDMA2
  Schedule Days: 14
  Instructions: Calculate % values out of range; if <=20% out of range = GDMA1; if >20% out of range = GDMA2; alternative triggers: >5 abnormal in 7 days, >2 elevated at same meal over 2 weeks
  Guideline: ACOG Practice Bulletin 190

INT-007:
  Type: MEDICATION
  Description: Insulin therapy for GDMA2 (first-line pharmacotherapy)
  Medication Code: 86009
  Dosage: 0.7-1.0 units/kg/day total, 50% basal/50% prandial
  Frequency: QID
  Schedule Days: 14
  Instructions: Lispro or aspart for prandial; NPH or glargine for basal; titrate based on glucose patterns; anticipate increasing requirements
  Guideline: ACOG Insulin First-Line Recommendation

INT-008:
  Type: MEDICATION
  Description: Metformin as alternative for GDMA2 if insulin declined
  Medication Code: 861007
  Dosage: Start 500mg, titrate to max 2500mg daily
  Frequency: BID
  Schedule Days: 14
  Instructions: Counsel placental transfer; 50% will need supplemental insulin; contraindicated in preeclampsia/IUGR risk; expeditious transition to insulin if inadequate
  Guideline: SMFM Metformin Alternative Statement

INT-009:
  Type: MONITORING
  Description: GDMA1 fetal surveillance - NO routine testing unless risk factors
  Schedule Days: 224
  Instructions: No routine NST/BPP for well-controlled GDMA1; initiate testing at 32 weeks ONLY if: macrosomia, FGR, polyhydramnios, preeclampsia, prior stillbirth, or suboptimal control develops
  Guideline: SMFM Choosing Wisely, ACOG Practice Bulletin 190

INT-010:
  Type: MONITORING
  Description: GDMA2 well-controlled fetal surveillance - 1-2x weekly at 32 weeks
  Procedure Code: 59025
  Schedule Days: 224
  Instructions: Begin NST or BPP at 32 weeks; frequency 1-2x weekly; options: weekly NST + weekly modified BPP, OR twice weekly NST, OR weekly BPP
  Guideline: SMFM Pharmacotherapy Surveillance Recommendation

INT-011:
  Type: MONITORING
  Description: GDMA2 poorly controlled fetal surveillance - 2x weekly minimum
  Procedure Code: 59025
  Schedule Days: 224
  Instructions: Poorly controlled = HbA1c >8.5% or >20% values out of range on meds; NST/BPP 2x weekly minimum; consider 3x weekly if severe; earlier start if complications
  Guideline: ACOG High-Risk Fetal Surveillance

INT-012:
  Type: MONITORING
  Description: Growth ultrasound schedule based on GDM class
  Procedure Code: 76816
  Schedule Days: 224
  Instructions: GDMA1: baseline at diagnosis, repeat only if indicated, consider 36-37wk if concerns. GDMA2: initial at 28-32wk, every 4 weeks, final 36-37wk. GDMA2 poor: q4wk plus targeted scans at 32, 36, 37+ wk
  Guideline: ACOG Fetal Growth Monitoring

INT-013:
  Type: PROCEDURE
  Description: Delivery timing based on GDM class and control
  Schedule Days: 273
  Instructions: GDMA1 well-controlled: 39 0/7 to 40 6/7 weeks. GDMA2 well-controlled: 39 0/7 to 39 6/7 weeks. GDMA2 poorly controlled: 37 0/7 to 38 6/7 weeks. Earlier (34-36 6/7) only for failed inpatient optimization, abnormal surveillance, or complications
  Guideline: ACOG Delivery Timing by GDM Class

INT-014:
  Type: PROCEDURE
  Description: Intrapartum glucose management
  Schedule Days: 273
  Instructions: Monitor glucose q1-2hr during labor; target 70-110 mg/dL. GDMA1: monitor only, treat if hyperglycemia. GDMA2 insulin: IV insulin protocol or sliding scale. GDMA2 metformin: hold, insulin PRN
  Guideline: ACOG Intrapartum Glucose Management

INT-015:
  Type: EDUCATION
  Description: Breastfeeding initiation and support
  Schedule Days: 273
  Instructions: Encourage early and frequent breastfeeding; reduces neonatal hypoglycemia; may reduce maternal T2DM risk
  Guideline: ADA Breastfeeding Recommendations

INT-016:
  Type: PROCEDURE
  Description: Postpartum 75-gram OGTT at 4-12 weeks
  Procedure Code: 82951
  Schedule Days: 301
  Instructions: Use non-pregnancy criteria. Normal: FPG <100 AND 2hr <140. Prediabetes: FPG 100-125 OR 2hr 140-199. T2DM: FPG >=126 OR 2hr >=200
  Guideline: ACOG/ADA Postpartum Screening

INT-017:
  Type: FOLLOW_UP
  Description: Long-term diabetes screening every 1-3 years lifelong
  Procedure Code: 83036
  Schedule Days: 365
  Instructions: If postpartum OGTT normal, screen every 1-3 years for minimum 10 years; lifelong recommended given >50% lifetime T2DM risk
  Guideline: ADA Long-Term Surveillance

INT-018:
  Type: EDUCATION
  Description: Diabetes prevention counseling for prediabetes
  Schedule Days: 301
  Instructions: Intensive lifestyle intervention reduces T2DM risk 58%; consider metformin if BMI >=35; annual screening if prediabetes
  Guideline: ADA Diabetes Prevention Program

INT-019:
  Type: EDUCATION
  Description: Preconception counseling for future pregnancies
  Schedule Days: 365
  Instructions: 40-84% GDM recurrence risk; optimize weight and metabolic health; if T2DM diagnosed, achieve HbA1c <6.5% before conception
  Guideline: ADA Preconception Care Standards

[CLINICAL CONTENT]
--- CLASSIFICATION ---
GDMA1 (Class A1 - Diet-Controlled):
- Abnormal OGTT meeting diagnostic criteria
- Fasting glucose <105 mg/dL AND 2-hour postprandial <120 mg/dL on lifestyle alone
- Managed with MNT + exercise without pharmacotherapy
- <=20% of glucose values out of target range
- ICD-10-CM: O24.410

GDMA2 (Class A2 - Medication-Requiring):
- Fasting glucose >=105 mg/dL AND/OR postprandial >=120 mg/dL (2-hr) or >=140 mg/dL (1-hr)
- Requires insulin or oral hypoglycemic agents
- >20% of glucose values remain out of range despite 1-2 weeks of MNT
- ICD-10-CM: O24.414 (insulin), O24.415 (oral agents)

--- GLYCEMIC CONTROL DEFINITIONS ---
Well-Controlled:
- <=1 occasion of FPG >=95 OR 2-hr PP >=120 during monitoring period
- <25% time above range (>140 mg/dL) if using CGM
- >=80% of glucose values at target

Suboptimally Controlled:
- >1 occasion of FPG >=95 OR 2-hr PP >=120
- Associated with higher postpartum T2DM risk

Poorly Controlled:
- HbA1c >8.5%
- Persistent hyperglycemia despite pharmacotherapy
- >20% values out of range on current regimen
- 3-8 fold increased congenital anomaly risk if early pregnancy

--- A1 TO A2 ESCALATION TRIGGERS ---
Primary: >20% of blood glucose values out of target range after 1-2 weeks MNT

Alternative triggers (any one):
- >5 abnormal values in any 7-day period
- >2 elevated values at same meal during 2-week period (>10 mg/dL above target)
- >=50% of values in a given week elevated above target

--- FETAL SURVEILLANCE BY CLASS ---
GDMA1 (Diet-Controlled):
- Routine antenatal testing: NOT indicated
- SMFM Choosing Wisely: "Do not perform routine fetal surveillance in patients with
  appropriate glucose control via dietary modifications alone"
- Exceptions requiring testing at 32 weeks: macrosomia, FGR, polyhydramnios, preeclampsia,
  prior stillbirth, suboptimal control
- Growth US: Not routine; consider 36-37 weeks if any concerns

GDMA2 Well-Controlled:
- Start testing: 32 weeks (earlier if renal/vascular disease or IUGR)
- NST/BPP: 1-2x weekly
- Growth US: Every 4 weeks, final at 36-37 weeks

GDMA2 Poorly Controlled:
- Start testing: 32 weeks (or earlier if complications)
- NST/BPP: 2x weekly MINIMUM; consider 3x weekly if severe
- Growth US: Every 4 weeks PLUS targeted scans at 32, 36, 37+ weeks
- Consider hospitalization for optimization

--- DELIVERY TIMING BY CLASS ---
| Classification           | Delivery Timing          |
|--------------------------|--------------------------|
| GDMA1 well-controlled    | 39 0/7 - 40 6/7 weeks    |
| GDMA2 well-controlled    | 39 0/7 - 39 6/7 weeks    |
| GDMA2 suboptimally ctrl  | 38 0/7 - 39 0/7 weeks    |
| GDMA2 poorly controlled  | 37 0/7 - 38 6/7 weeks    |
| Any GDM + complications  | Individualized, earlier  |

Earlier delivery (34-36 6/7 weeks) reserved for:
- Failed in-hospital glycemic optimization
- Abnormal fetal surveillance
- Fetal growth restriction
- Preeclampsia/severe hypertension

--- PHARMACOTHERAPY ---
First-Line: INSULIN (preferred - does not cross placenta)
- Rapid-acting: Lispro (Humalog) or Aspart (NovoLog) for prandial
- Basal: NPH, Glargine (Lantus), or Detemir (Levemir)
- Starting dose: 0.7-1.0 units/kg/day, divided 50% basal/50% prandial
- Titrate based on glucose patterns

Alternative: METFORMIN (if insulin declined)
- Crosses placenta (cord levels = maternal levels)
- 50% will require supplemental insulin
- Contraindicated: preeclampsia, hypertension, IUGR risk
- Start 500mg daily, max 2500mg divided

NOT Recommended: Glyburide (inferior outcomes)

--- POSTPARTUM SCREENING ---
75-gram OGTT at 4-12 weeks postpartum using NON-PREGNANCY criteria:
- Normal: FPG <100 mg/dL AND 2-hr <140 mg/dL
- Prediabetes: FPG 100-125 mg/dL OR 2-hr 140-199 mg/dL
- Type 2 Diabetes: FPG >=126 mg/dL OR 2-hr >=200 mg/dL

Long-term: Screen every 1-3 years lifelong (>50% lifetime T2DM risk)
